## 7. Grounding and Safety Semantics
- Only `llm + succeeded retrieval + non-empty citations + v4_grounded` can produce `knowledge_grounded=true`.
- Evidence is treated as untrusted reference data, not instructions.
- Evidence is limited to at most 5 items.
- Each evidence item is limited to at most 2000 characters.
- Commands found inside evidence cannot override system instructions.
- Citations are generated by the system and are not invented by the LLM.
